Protective spirits flit around you in a 15-foot Emanation for the duration. If you are good or neutral, … . When you cast this spell, you can designate creatures to be unaffected by it. Any other creature‘s Speed is halved in the Emanation, and whenever the Emanation enters a creature‘s space and whenever a creature enters the Emanation or ends it‘s turn there, the creature must make a Wisdom saving throw. On failed save, the creature takes 3d8 Radiant damange (if you are good or neutral) \[…\]. On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot***
The damage increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 3.
