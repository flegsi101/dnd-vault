---
level: 3
casting-time: bonus action
range: 60 feet
components:
  - verbal
  - sensory
duration: instant
prepared: true
---
Up to six creatures of your choice that you can see within range regain Hipt Points equal to 2d4 plus your spellcasting modifier.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot***
The healing increases by 1d4 for each spell slot level above 3.
